Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

1. Summary

Autoclip collects as little as possible. It stores the access tokens needed to post to your account, processes videos on the operator’s own computer, and does not sell or share your data.

2. What Autoclip accesses

  • Authorization tokens. When you connect TikTok or YouTube, the platform gives Autoclip an access token and a refresh token for the permissions you granted (for TikTok, video.publish). These tokens are used only to publish the clips you ask it to publish.
  • Videos and clips. Source videos, transcripts, and generated clips are processed and stored on the operator’s computer. A clip is sent to TikTok or YouTube only when you post it.

Autoclip does not read your existing videos, followers, messages, or other personal data from your accounts.

3. How data is used

Data is used only to create clips and to publish them to your account on your request. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or analytics.

4. Sharing

Autoclip does not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. Data is exchanged only with TikTok and Google when you post a clip, and under their own privacy policies.

5. Storage and security

Tokens and files are kept on the operator’s own device and are not uploaded to any server run by Autoclip. The site you are reading uses no cookies and no tracking scripts.

6. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until you revoke access or ask for them to be deleted. You can revoke Autoclip’s access at any time in your TikTok or Google account settings. To request deletion of any data related to you, write to simossass@gmail.com.

7. Children

Autoclip is not intended for anyone below the minimum age required by TikTok or Google to hold an account.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated. The latest version is always on this page, with its date.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy: simossass@gmail.com.
